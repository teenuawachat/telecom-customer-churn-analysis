# 📶 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

## 📖 Project Overview
Analyzed telecom customer data using Power BI to identify churn patterns across contract types, tenure, payment methods, and monthly charges. Built an interactive dashboard to support customer retention decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem
Customer churn directly impacts revenue and business growth. This project analyzes customer characteristics and service usage to identify factors associated with churn and support retention strategies.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard Development, Data Modeling, DAX
- **Power Query** – Data Cleaning & Transformation

## 📊 Analysis Areas
- Overall customer churn rate
- Churn by contract type
- Churn by tenure
- Churn by payment method
- Monthly charges by churn status

## 💡 Key Insights
- Month-to-month contracts showed the highest churn rate.
- Customers with shorter tenure were more likely to churn.
- Electronic check users experienced the highest churn.
- Churned customers had higher average monthly charges than retained customers.

## 📈 Business Recommendations
- Encourage long-term contracts to improve customer retention.
- Focus retention efforts on new customers during their first year.
- Review pricing and service quality for high-risk customer segments.
- Monitor payment preferences to identify customers with higher churn risk.
